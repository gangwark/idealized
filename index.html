Statics Modeling Studio

Real object → idealized model → free-body diagram → equilibrium

About this prototype

Statics Modeling Studio helps you turn a photographed object into an idealized engineering statics model. The AI proposes; you decide; statics determines the consequences. It extends the Statics Scavenger Hunt activity developed at Wentworth Institute of Technology by Kapil Gangwar and Gloria Ma.

What works in this version

  • The full 2D workflow for a single rigid body, with the support types of Table 5-1 plus rough (frictional) contact.
  • Deterministic equation generation and solving, with checks for indeterminacy, improper constraints, mechanisms, slack cables, lift-off, and slip.
  • Guided, Practice, Challenge, and Exploration modes.

What is not connected yet

  • Live AI image analysis. The three worked examples use fixed, pre-written AI proposals. For your own photos you build the model yourself.
  • 3D solving, multi-body frames and machines, and trusses.

Privacy

Photos stay in this browser tab. They are resized on your device, which also removes embedded location data, and they are not uploaded anywhere. Exported model files do not include photos. Closing the tab discards everything that you have not exported.

When live AI analysis is added, photos will be sent to an AI provider through a course server, and this notice will say so before anything is sent.

Safety: never climb, lean out, or enter restricted areas to photograph a structure.
